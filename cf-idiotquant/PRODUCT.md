# IdiotQuant product context

## Users

Individual investors researching Korean and US equities, from first-time screeners to experienced investors comparing financial data.

## Product purpose

Help people discover companies, compare financial evidence, and understand how valuation estimates depend on their assumptions.

## Main journey

Discover a company → compare it with alternatives → review financial and valuation evidence → save it to a watchlist → revisit changes.

## Product constraints

- Present market data, financial periods, currencies, and valuation assumptions clearly.
- Never present an estimate as a guaranteed price, recommendation, or official delisting decision.
- Preserve the user's saved watchlist and make loading failures recoverable.
- Design data-heavy screens for narrow phones and keyboard use as well as desktop.
